# Practice creative writing through prompts.

# Prompt 1

    In about 300 words,  write a story about why friendship can be more important than romance 
# Model output

    When Maya’s long-term relationship ended, she expected heartbreak. What she didn’t expect was silence.

    For years, her world had revolved around one person—weekend plans, daily calls, future dreams. When it ended, the quiet in her apartment felt heavier than the breakup itself. But then, her phone buzzed.

    It was Tolu.

    No dramatic speech. No “I told you so.” Just: *“I’m outside. Bring snacks.”*

    That night, they sat on the floor surrounded by pizza boxes, laughing at old school memories. Tolu didn’t try to fix Maya’s pain or turn it into a lesson. She just stayed. The next morning, she returned with coffee. The day after, she dragged Maya out for a walk. Slowly, the ache softened.

    Maya began to notice something. Romance had filled her life with intensity—big gestures, passionate arguments, constant reassurance. But friendship was steady. It didn’t demand performance. It didn’t threaten to disappear after one disagreement. It simply endured.

    Weeks later, when Maya got a job offer in another city, it wasn’t a romantic partner she called first. It was Tolu. They talked through fears, budgets, and possibilities. Tolu reminded her who she was before the relationship and who she could still become.

    That was when Maya understood: romance can be beautiful, but friendship is foundational. Romance may begin with fireworks, but friendship is the quiet light that stays on when everything else goes dark.

    Years later, Maya would fall in love again. But she would never build her whole world around one person. She had learned that while romance can change your life, friendship can hold it together.


# Prpmpt 2

    write a 300 words essay discussing the significant scientific, communication and technological innovations of space exploration

# Model output 

    Space exploration has driven remarkable advancements in science, communication, and technology, transforming how humanity understands the universe and interacts on Earth. Scientifically, missions to the Moon, Mars, and beyond have expanded our knowledge of planetary formation, geology, and atmospheric dynamics. Instruments aboard spacecraft, such as spectrometers and rovers, allow scientists to analyze soil, ice, and rock samples remotely, providing insights into the potential for life beyond Earth and the history of our solar system. Observatories like the Hubble Space Telescope have revealed galaxies billions of light-years away, refining theories about the origin and evolution of the cosmos.

    In communication, space exploration has accelerated innovations that connect the planet. Satellite technology, initially developed for space missions, underpins global positioning systems (GPS), weather forecasting, and international telecommunications. Deep-space communication systems, such as NASA’s Deep Space Network, enable real-time data exchange with spacecraft millions of kilometers away, pushing the boundaries of signal processing and long-distance transmission. These advancements have applications in disaster management, internet connectivity in remote areas, and global monitoring of environmental changes.

    Technologically, the challenges of surviving and operating in space have led to breakthroughs with wide-ranging impacts. Materials engineered to withstand extreme temperatures and radiation improve everything from aviation to medical devices. Life-support systems, water recycling, and energy-efficient propulsion technologies developed for spacecraft contribute to sustainable living solutions on Earth. Robotics, AI, and autonomous systems, refined through space missions, now aid industries such as manufacturing, agriculture, and healthcare.

    Overall, space exploration is not just about reaching distant worlds; it is a catalyst for innovation. Scientific discovery, enhanced communication networks, and cutting-edge technologies developed for space missions have reshaped daily life and expanded humanity’s potential. As exploration continues, these innovations will drive further progress, making space a frontier not only for curiosity but also for practical advancement.
