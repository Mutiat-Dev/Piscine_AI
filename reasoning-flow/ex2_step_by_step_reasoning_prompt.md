Objective: Use structured prompting to encourage reasoning.

# The prompt without reasoning 

"Calculate the factorial of 5".

# Model output

    The factorial of 5 is **120**.

# Prompting the model: "Let's think step by step before answering."
# Model output

Step 1: The factorial of a number 
    n
    n is:

    n!=n×(n−1)×(n−2)×⋯×1

So:

    5!=5×4×3×2×1

Step 2: Multiply step by step

    5×4=20

    20×3=60

    60×2=120

    120×1=120

Step 3: Final Answer

    5!=120
   

# Comparison

    The output without the reasoning instruction is quite different from the output where the instruction is stated. It answers the question directly and straight to the point, the second output shows the formular for factorial, inputed the number in question ans showed the steps before reaching the final answer. It helps anybody to understand how it works and simplify it better.
