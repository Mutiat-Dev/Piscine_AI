# Control tone and style with explicit instructions.
# The prompts
 "Write an email inviting a candidate to an interview".

 # Model output of a professional tone

        Dear [Candidate’s Name],

    Thank you for your application for the [Position Title] role at [Company Name]. We are pleased to inform you that you have been shortlisted for an interview.

    We would like to invite you to attend an interview on [Date] at [Time]. The interview will take place [at our office located at / virtually via (platform name)]. The discussion will last approximately [duration] and will provide an opportunity for us to learn more about your experience and for you to ask any questions about the role.

    Please confirm your availability by replying to this email. If the proposed time is not convenient, kindly let us know your availability so we can arrange an alternative.

    We look forward to speaking with you.

    Best regards,
    Katibi M
    [Your Position]
    [Company Name]
    [Contact Information]

# Model output of a friendly tone

     Hi [Friend’s Name],

    I hope you’re doing well! I wanted to let you know that you’ve been shortlisted for an interview for the [Position Title] role at [Company Name].

    The interview is scheduled for [Date] at [Time], and it will be [in-person at / online via (platform name)]. It should last about [duration], and it’ll be a great chance to chat about your experience and the role.

    Can you let me know if that time works for you? If not, we can figure out another slot.

    Looking forward to seeing you shine!

    Cheers,
    Katibi

# Reflection

    The first email is a professional email that's used in any business or official setting and is staright to the point. The second email is more casual and it feels like a text message rather than a formal email.
