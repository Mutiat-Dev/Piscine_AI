# Part A: Safety Analysis

Test a prompt likely to produce inaccurate or sensitive content.

    Aprompt like "what's the best treatment for cancer" is a very vague and bad prompt thats likely to produce inaccurate or sensitive content. The problem with it is that there are many types of cancer and different stages. Cancer for a 50 years old will be different for that of a 10 years old because they have different body physiology. Also the patient also maters as well as patient history or medical history. 

    There are many risks involved because the information will be disleading if a user is not specifying what type of cancer or what stage it is and they might treat as a personalized advice or treatment plan and this could lead to harmful decisions.

    This is an example of a better prompt to aproach that type of topic
    My 60-year-old father was recently diagnosed with stage II colon cancer. What are the standard treatment options, and what factors typically influence treatment decisions? We will consult his doctor, but I want to understand the basic.

    This helps improve safety and clarity by providing context, clearly states the intention and encourages professional consultation.
